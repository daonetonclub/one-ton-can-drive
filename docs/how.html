How it works

Four steps, about fifteen seconds.

1

Bring cans to school

Any non-perishable food. Drop them in any One Ton bin on campus. There's no separate bin per grade.

2

Scan the QR code

One code for the whole school. It's on the sign next to every bin.

3

Log what you brought

Grade, advisory, your name, how many cans. Next time, your phone remembers you, so it's just the number.

4

Watch the ton fill up

Your cans count toward the school total and toward every contest running that day.

📦
Where it goes: every can goes to a local food bank that gets food to families in our area. The club picks our partner together, and we will post the name here once we do.
